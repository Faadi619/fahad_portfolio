Featured project

Live · Saudi Arabia · Arabic-first

AMA: an Arabic-first marketplace for Arabian horses

About 11 weeks from start to full scope, against a plan of 7 months part-time.

The problem

A Saudi client needed a trusted place to buy and sell Arabian horses online. Buyers' money had to be held safely until the horse arrived, every member had to be verified, and the product had to work in Arabic first, not be translated into it.

What I built

The whole product, on a fixed-scope contract, as 18 modules:

  • Browsing, listings, selling and accounts
  • Escrow orders and buyer–seller messaging
  • A services marketplace for vets, transporters and farriers
  • Identity verification for every member
  • An admin console for registrations, disputes, refunds and payouts
  • PDPL data export and erasure requests

It's fully right-to-left, with light and dark themes and Arabic-Indic numerals. The backend is designed so a mobile app can be added later.

How

  • Only the server can write a financial record. Every payment, escrow and ledger movement goes through database functions.
  • Payments run through Tap with mada and cards, including 3-D Secure. Webhooks are HMAC-verified and processed only once, and refunds are automated.
  • Staging and live are separate. Every release passes unit, SQL, end-to-end, visual and accessibility tests, plus a security review.

Result

The full scope was delivered between 10 September and late November 2026, about 11 weeks, and the marketplace is live.
