About

I write Flutter, and I have spent the last five years responsible for what happens around it. As CTO at AppSpot Software Solutions I led a team of four developers, set the engineering standards we built against — MVVM, Clean Architecture, feature-first structuring — and mentored the team on state management.

The work I am proudest of sits where the app meets something physical or awkward: forecourt controllers streaming telemetry over a retry-heavy protocol, iOS Family Controls enforcing app limits, a BLE link to an ESP32 reading live diagnostic codes out of a car. That is also where the interesting decisions live — what to isolate on staging, what to keep out of the mobile binary entirely, when removing an API is the better call than adding one.

I am comfortable owning the mobile layer completely while directing engineers on system design, data modeling, and integration architecture. I am looking for senior Flutter roles, or a CTO or Head of Engineering position with real technical ownership rather than a purely managerial title.
